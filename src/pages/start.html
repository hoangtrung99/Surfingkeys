Surfingkeys is ready

Beyond Surfingkeys' Vim-like keys, this build adds three things. Each works on this page too.

Command palette

Search your open tabs, open a URL or search the web, and run any action by name.

Inside pages opens it too.

Tab switcher

Hold Alt and press Q to step through your recent tabs, then release Alt to switch. Add Shift to step back.

Themes

Ten colour themes for Surfingkeys' panels, hints and this page. ;T or :theme nord changes it from any page.

New tab page

Also in this build: a start page with your bookmarks bar, its folders opening as dropdowns, and your top sites, where Surfingkeys' keys work the moment it opens. Make it every new tab (in Helium), open it when the browser starts, or both.
